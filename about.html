Agronomy + Artificial Intelligence

Accessible AI for Plant Health

CropSentry AI provides an accessible image classifier for bell pepper, potato, and tomato leaf classes.

Our Driving Purpose

Making Leaf Classification Accessible

Identifying leaf conditions can be challenging. CropSentry provides a quick model-based class prediction for supported bell pepper, potato, and tomato leaf images.

CropSentry AI uses a trained image classifier to return leaf-class scores for bell pepper, potato, and tomato. Its output is not a diagnosis, severity assessment, or treatment recommendation.

Core Impact Pillars

Early Pathogen Interception

Detect foliar pathogens at asymptomatic or early lesion stages before irreversible boll rot occurs.

Targeted Input Optimization

Reduce indiscriminate chemical fungicide overdosing through precise, verified dosage recommendations.

Multilingual Democratization

Removing language barriers with native English, Hindi, and Marathi interfaces and voice support.
