Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding AI accuracy, offline capabilities, and data security.

How accurate is the CropSentry AI computer vision model?
The trained MobileNetV2 model achieved 86.29% accuracy on its 3,101-image held-out test set. Performance varies by class; the returned class score is not a measure of diagnosis certainty.
What should I do if the AI confidence rating is below 70%?
Low confidence often occurs from extreme camera blur, heavy shadows, or leaves covered in dry dust. We recommend retaking the photograph against a plain background under direct daylight, or utilizing our 'Compare Leaves' tool.
Is the disease diagnosis service free for farmers?
Yes, CropSentry is 100% free and open-access for smallholder cultivators and students. We believe precision agronomic tools should be universally accessible.
Can I use the app without an internet connection?
Once loaded in your browser, key diagnostic guides and previous scans remain cached locally. Our offline on-device Edge ML version is launching in Phase 1 (Q3 2026).
